1. Mission Statement

The Privacy Program of Acme Inc exists to protect the personal data of all individuals whose information we collect, process, or store. Our mission is to embed privacy into every aspect of our operations, maintain compliance with applicable data protection regulations, and foster a culture of transparency and accountability.

1.1 Core Principles

  1. Privacy by Design — Build privacy protections into products and processes from the outset

  2. Data Minimisation — Collect only what is necessary for the stated purpose

  3. Transparency — Clearly communicate data practices to all stakeholders

  4. Accountability — Maintain demonstrable compliance with all applicable regulations

  5. Security — Protect personal data with appropriate technical and organisational measures

2. Scope

This charter applies to:

  • All Acme Inc employees, contractors, and temporary staff

  • All business units and departments

  • All systems and applications that process personal data

  • All third-party vendors and sub-processors

  • All data subjects whose information is processed

9. Incident Response Integration

The Privacy Program integrates with the Incident Response Plan:

  1. Detection — Privacy incidents are reported through the same channels as security incidents

  2. Assessment — DPO assesses whether a personal data breach has occurred

  3. Notification — Authority notification within 72 hours (GDPR Art. 33); data subject notification without undue delay if high risk (GDPR Art. 34)

  4. Remediation — Technical fixes, process improvements, updated DPIAs

  5. Lessons learned — Post-incident review with Privacy Steering Committee
