3. Management Commitment

The senior leadership of Acme Inc hereby commits to the following:

3.1 Information Security (ISO 27001 Clause 5.1)

  • Leadership and Direction: Ensuring the information security policy and objectives are established and compatible with the strategic direction of the organization

  • Integration: Ensuring information security requirements are integrated into the organization's processes

  • Resources: Ensuring adequate resources are available for the ISMS

  • Communication: Communicating the importance of effective information security management

  • Outcomes: Ensuring the ISMS achieves its intended outcomes

  • Continual Improvement: Directing and supporting persons to contribute to the effectiveness of the ISMS

3.2 Data Protection (GDPR Art. 5(2) Accountability)

  • Lawful Processing: Ensuring all personal data processing has a valid legal basis

  • Transparency: Maintaining clear and accessible privacy notices for all data subjects

  • Data Minimization: Collecting only the data necessary for specified purposes

  • Accuracy: Taking reasonable steps to ensure personal data is accurate and up to date

  • Storage Limitation: Retaining personal data only for as long as necessary

  • Integrity and Confidentiality: Implementing appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data

  • Accountability: Being able to demonstrate compliance with all data protection principles

6. Breach Response Commitment

In the event of a personal data breach, management commits to:

  1. 72-hour notification to the relevant supervisory authority (GDPR Art. 33)

  2. Undue delay notification to affected data subjects when the breach poses a high risk (GDPR Art. 34)

  3. Full cooperation with supervisory authorities during investigations

  4. Post-incident review within 14 days of any security incident

  5. Remediation of root causes and implementation of preventive measures

  6. Transparent communication with affected parties throughout the incident lifecycle
