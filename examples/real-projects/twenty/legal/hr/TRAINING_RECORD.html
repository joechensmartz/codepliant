3. Core Training Topics

3.1 Data Protection Basics

  • What constitutes personal data and special category data
  • The six GDPR principles (lawfulness, fairness, transparency, purpose limitation, data minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation, integrity & confidentiality, accountability)
  • Lawful bases for processing (consent, contract, legal obligation, vital interests, public task, legitimate interests)
  • Data subject rights overview (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection)
  • Privacy by design and by default (Art. 25)
  • International data transfers and safeguards
  • [Your Company Name]'s role as data controller/processor

3.2 Breach Procedures

  • Definition of a personal data breach
  • How to recognise a potential breach
  • Internal escalation procedure (report within 4 hours to DPO)
  • 72-hour supervisory authority notification requirement (Art. 33)
  • Data subject notification requirements (Art. 34)
  • Breach documentation and record-keeping
  • Post-breach review and lessons learned

3.3 DSAR Handling

  • Recognising a Data Subject Access Request (verbal and written)
  • Identity verification procedures
  • Response timeline (1 month, extendable to 3 months for complex requests)
  • Information to include in responses
  • Handling requests for erasure, rectification, and restriction
  • When and how to refuse a request (manifestly unfounded or excessive)
  • Escalation to the DPO

3.4 AI & Automated Decision-Making

  • Transparency obligations for AI systems (Art. 22)
  • Right to human intervention in automated decisions
  • AI training data privacy considerations
  • Bias detection and fairness requirements
  • EU AI Act risk classification awareness

3.5 Payment Data Protection

  • PCI DSS fundamentals and cardholder data handling
  • Tokenisation and encryption requirements
  • Secure payment processing procedures
  • Incident handling for payment data breaches

3.7 Authentication & Access Data
