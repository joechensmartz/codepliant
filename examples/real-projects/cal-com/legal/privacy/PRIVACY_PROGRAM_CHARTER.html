9. Incident Response Integration

The Privacy Program integrates with the Incident Response Plan:

  1. Detection — Privacy incidents are reported through the same channels as security incidents
  2. Assessment — DPO assesses whether a personal data breach has occurred
  3. Notification — Authority notification within 72 hours (GDPR Art. 33); data subject notification without undue delay if high risk (GDPR Art. 34)
  4. Remediation — Technical fixes, process improvements, updated DPIAs
  5. Lessons learned — Post-incident review with Privacy Steering Committee
